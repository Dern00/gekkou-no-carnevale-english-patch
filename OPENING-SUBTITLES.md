# Opening subtitles

In v1.1, click **Install patch** to add the subtitles automatically. The installer uses a portable renderer and the original opening in your game folder. The lyrics appear in romanized Japanese at the top and English at the bottom.

The timed subtitles come from **Project 5’s December 2020 translation extras**. The renderer checks the stock `mpeg\OP.mpg`, then combines its video and audio with the subtitles. The original movie is backed up in the game folder. **Restore original files** restores the movie alongside the other game files.

If you used the v1.0 optional opening helper, v1.1 recognizes its subtitle movie. Use that helper’s restore command if you want to undo its separate installation. [v1.0 opening helper instructions](https://github.com/Dern00/gekkou-no-carnevale-english-patch/blob/v1.0/OPENING-SUBTITLES.md).
